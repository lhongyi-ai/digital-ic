# Third-party sources and publication decisions

This private research snapshot does not grant a blanket license for every included item. Original project code has not been assigned a new public license during this upload. Preserve upstream notices when using third-party material.

## Public experimental datasets

- **MIMII public 1.0**: Harsh Purohit, Ryo Tanabe, Kenji Ichige, Takashi Endo, Yuki Nikaido, Kaori Suefusa, and Yohei Kawaguchi; Hitachi, Ltd. The [official dataset record](https://zenodo.org/records/3384388) specifies [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Bulk downloaded PCM is omitted to keep source data at its authoritative distribution point. Manifests, source-member names, download receipts, and numerical experimental outputs are retained.
- Two short MIMII fan-00 excerpts, `artifacts/known-recording-walkthrough/normal.wav` and `fault.wav`, and MIMII-derived replay inputs remain as necessary demonstration/verification material under CC BY-SA 4.0. They derive from `fan/id_00/normal/00000304.wav` and `fan/id_00/abnormal/00000094.wav`; the walkthrough selects channel 0 and the 156-window span, without independent gain normalization or denoising. File-specific origins and transformations for other replay inputs are recorded in their manifests. Attribution is to the dataset creators above; no endorsement is implied. See the [MIMII paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.09347).
- **MIMII DUE**: Ryo Tanabe, Harsh Purohit, Kota Dohi, Takashi Endo, Yuki Nikaido, Toshiki Nakamura, and Yohei Kawaguchi; Hitachi, Ltd. The [official record](https://zenodo.org/records/4740355) specifies [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). Downloaded raw audio/PCM is omitted. Retained source-derived feature arrays are research artifacts subject to the applicable dataset conditions; inclusion does not confer unrestricted commercial rights. See the [DUE paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.02702).
- **CWRU Bearing Data Center**: the [official site](https://engineering.case.edu/bearingdatacenter/welcome) provides the bearing-test records used in the historical vibration experiments. Complete `.mat` downloads are omitted, with exact source channels and SHA256 values retained in `data/cwru/manifest.json`. Small project test vectors and the simulation demonstration retain their source provenance. No new dataset license is asserted by this repository.

## Vendored code and optional pretrained weights

- **EfficientAT**, Florian Schmid, upstream commit `a425fdce92572e602a1d5634799bd9f1f2efa806`: English source is included under its [MIT license](https://github.com/fschmid56/EfficientAT/blob/main/LICENSE), preserved at `external/EfficientAT/LICENSE`. Downloaded `mn10_as` weights and upstream demo media are omitted; use the upstream resources instructions and `external/EfficientAT.snapshot.json` to restore and verify the checkpoint.
- **PANNs**, Qiuqiang Kong and contributors, source commit `d2f4b8c18eab44737fcc0de1248ae21eb43f6aa4`: the two saved source files are included with the upstream [MIT license](https://github.com/qiuqiangkong/audioset_tagging_cnn/blob/master/LICENSE.MIT), preserved at `third_party/panns/LICENSE.MIT`. The optional CNN6 checkpoint is omitted. Its exact source URL, size, MD5, and SHA256 are recorded in `third_party/panns/source.json`. The weight-distribution license was not independently confirmed during this publication review; do not infer it solely from the code license.

The original trained project models, fixed-point parameter exports, and saved experiment scores are retained, including failed candidates. External feature extractors remain clearly attributed and are not presented as original project inventions.
